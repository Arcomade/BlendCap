# Contributing

Thanks for your interest in BlendCap.

BlendCap is a commercial add-on, published here as open source to satisfy its license (GPL-3.0-or-later, which includes the AGPL-3.0 YOLO11 component). This repository exists so the source is available — it isn't an open community project, and it's maintained by one person alongside the paid product.

## Pull requests

I'm not accepting external pull requests. Development happens privately and is published here with each release, so a PR against this repo can't be merged into that flow. Please don't spend your time on one — I'd rather you didn't waste the effort.

You're welcome to fork and modify BlendCap for your own use under the terms of the license.

## Bugs, questions, and support

* **If you bought BlendCap:** support comes with your purchase. Message me through the product page you bought it from and include your OS, GPU, Blender version, and as much detail about the problem as you can. That's the fastest way to get help.
* **Building from source yourself:** that path is advanced and unsupported by design (see the [README](README.md)) — you're on your own there.

## Security

Please don't report security issues in public. Either use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting (the **Security** tab → **Report a vulnerability**, if enabled) or message me privately through the [store page](https://arcomade.gumroad.com/l/BlendCap). I'll get back to you as fast as I can.

Thanks for understanding — keeping this simple is what lets me put the time into the product itself.
