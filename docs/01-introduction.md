This guide covers everything from installing the add-on and its dependencies, to capturing a performance from video, refining it, and retargeting it onto your character to get it ready for a final animation pass.

## In this guide

New to BlendCap? Work through **Getting started** in order, then dip into the other sections as you need them.

**Getting started**
- [System Requirements](02-system-requirements.md): what you need to run BlendCap.
- [Installation](03-installation.md): install the add-on and set up its dependencies.
- [Quick Start](04-quick-start.md): your first capture, from start to finish.

**Capturing**
- [Capturing](05-capturing.md): turn a video into tracking data, including the capture settings and Preview.
- [Face Capture](06-face-capture.md): facial performance, ARKit blendshapes, and expression controls.
- [Hand & Finger Tracking](07-hand-tracking.md): how fingers are tracked and the controls that refine them.
- [Filming Tips](13-filming-tips.md): how to shoot footage that captures cleanly.

**Generating and retargeting**
- [BVH Generation and Post-Processing](08-bvh-generation-and-post-processing.md): turn your capture into a BVH armature and clean it up (smoothing, foot locking, depth, camera angle).
- [Retargeting](09-retargeting.md): transfer the armature onto your rig, in FK and IK.

**Reference**
- [BVH Library & Cache](10-bvh-library-and-cache.md): manage your captures and where they're stored.
- [Preferences](11-preferences.md): every add-on preference, explained.
- [Troubleshooting & FAQ](12-troubleshooting-and-faq.md): common questions and fixes.
- [Licensing & Credits](14-licensing-and-credits.md): licenses and attributions.

## Getting help

If something isn't working, the [Troubleshooting & FAQ](12-troubleshooting-and-faq.md) covers the common cases. For anything else, reach out through the store you bought BlendCap from: use the contact or message option on the BlendCap product page (for example on Superhive or Gumroad).
